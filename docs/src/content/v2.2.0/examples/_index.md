Fully-commented, runnable examples that demonstrate complete cellular automata implementations.
Each example can be copied and run immediately. Refer to them as templates for your own automata.

- **[Game of Life 2D](game-of-life-2d/)** — Classic Conway's Game of Life with Moore neighborhood.
- **[Heat Diffusion](heat-diffusion/)** — Multi-valued states and heat propagation with Von Neumann neighborhood.
- **[Game of Life 3D](game-of-life-3d/)** — Three-dimensional cellular automaton with Carter Bays' rules.
- **[Game of Life UI](game-of-life-ui/)** — Real-time Swing visualisation with glider and blinker patterns.
